Assemble a four-page submission

Put a one-page cover first, a two-page application second, and a supporting page last. Use Move up and Move down before merging. Open the downloaded PDF and verify the four-page count, reading order, orientation, and page sizes. These controls move complete files; they do not reorder individual pages inside a source PDF.

The PDF workflow guide explains the sequence and checks. The merge page includes downloadable synthetic source files and their actual merged output so you can compare a known example.

When another workflow is needed

Image conversion embeds pictures and does not perform OCR. Large or differently sized sources create large or differently sized pages. Crop, rotate, and resize copies before conversion when consistent output matters. Password-protected or damaged PDFs may fail; this site does not unlock them. Interactive forms, signatures, bookmarks, and other document features need inspection in the final reader. Keep the originals until the recipient's requirements are met.
