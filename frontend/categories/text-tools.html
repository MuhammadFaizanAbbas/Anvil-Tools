Choosing between text tools

Text Case Converter

Convert text to upper, lower, title, sentence, camel, snake, and kebab case. Check brand names, acronyms, and words with special capitalization manually after conversion.

Text Difference Checker

Compare two texts line by line and highlight additions and removals. Read added and removed lines together. Moving a line may appear as a removal and addition rather than a separate move.

Word & Character Counter

Count words, characters, sentences, and estimated reading time as you type. Compare the final count with the submission platform, especially when punctuation or languages without spaces are involved.
