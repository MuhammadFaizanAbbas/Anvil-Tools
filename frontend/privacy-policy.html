Privacy policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026.

What this site is

Anvil Tools ("we", "us") provides free browser-based tools at https://anviltools.vercel.app. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the site and the tools on it.

Information processed by the tools themselves

Most tools on this site (background remover, PDF merge, image to PDF, QR code generator, password generator, word counter, JSON formatter, Base64 tool, user agent generator, color palette generator, and unit converter) run entirely in your browser. Files and text you enter into these tools are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to our servers.

The temporary email tool works differently: it creates a real, active email inbox using a third-party email service (Guerrilla Mail) so that it can actually receive mail. Messages sent to that inbox pass through that service's infrastructure. Do not send anything sensitive to a temporary inbox, and review that provider's own terms if you want details of how they handle message data.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our server and any analytics or advertising scripts we use may automatically log standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and timestamps, for security, abuse prevention, and understanding how the site is used in aggregate.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses cookies for basic functionality and, once advertising is enabled, may use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and your options, at Google's page on how data is used when you use partner sites and apps.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and where required by law, we display a consent notice before non-essential cookies are set.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or serve personalized ads based on activity by users known to be under that age.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's cookies and local storage at any time. For the temporary email tool specifically, closing the tab clears your browser access. Provider messages follow Guerrilla Mail retention rules; generating a new address does not guarantee deletion at the provider.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@velloxtech.com.

Contact requests and email

When you submit our contact form, we store your name, email address, subject, message, and the related email delivery records in our Supabase database. Authorized administrators use these records to respond. Our SMTP provider processes the alert sent to faizan@velloxtech.com, your receipt, and replies. Contact details are used for support, not added to a marketing list. We use hashed IP and email rate-limit identifiers to limit form abuse.

To request access to or deletion of a support conversation, email info@velloxtech.com. Mailbox copies are separate from the website database.

Workspace accounts and temporary mail

Supabase verifies workspace passwords and access tokens. Authorized administrators create team accounts directly. The website stores the admin access token in tab session storage until its expiry; signing out clears it. Necessary session storage is separate from optional analytics and advertising preferences.

Temporary inbox credentials are stored server-side until expiry. Guerrilla Mail receives the inbox messages and, where required by its API, the client IP address and browser user agent. Inbox creation is limited by a hashed IP identifier: people sharing a network may share a quota. Never use these addresses for financial, medical, or other sensitive correspondence.

Local processing and network requests

Processing input locally means the tool operates on the text or file in your browser. The browser still requests page assets, fonts, libraries, and, for background removal, model files. Those requests expose ordinary connection information to the servers delivering them. Local processing should not be interpreted as a promise that visiting a page makes no network requests.

Optional usage measurement

Automatic browser analytics and advertising are currently disabled. The backend supports tool-view events that identify a tool without including its input text or files. Saving an analytics preference does not itself enable tracking in this version. The API uses a hashed client-address identifier to limit repeated requests. Preference storage lets the site remember your choice on subsequent visits.

Managing browser data

Use Privacy settings in the footer to review optional preferences. Clearing site data removes locally saved preferences and may end access to a workspace session. Closing a page or clearing browser data does not delete messages held by an email provider, support records in the database, or copies already delivered to a mailbox.

Support records and deletion requests

Support conversations can include the information you submit, administrator replies, delivery status, and operational records. Avoid attaching passwords, live access tokens, or private documents to a support request. If a small example is needed, replace personal information with sample values.

Contact info@velloxtech.com to ask about your support records or request deletion. Include enough information to locate the conversation. Verification may be needed before sharing or removing records. Provider-held messages and mailbox copies are separate from the site database; this policy does not promise a fixed deletion schedule for every service.

Questions about this policy

Does rejecting optional analytics disable the tools?

Optional analytics preferences are separate from the tool functions and necessary workspace sessions.

Does the site keep a history of my converted files?

The browser tools do not provide a server-side file history. Save outputs you need before closing the page. Temporary email and support conversations follow the separate processing described above.
