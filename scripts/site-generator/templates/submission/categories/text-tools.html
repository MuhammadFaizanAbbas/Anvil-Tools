What the numbers and edits mean

The counter uses whitespace for word boundaries and UTF-16 units for characters. Its 200-word-per-minute estimate is for rough silent reading, not spoken timing or comprehension. Case conversion applies character rules, not editorial judgment. The difference checker compares plain text by line and ignores fonts and page layout. Keep the original and review the final text where it will be published.
