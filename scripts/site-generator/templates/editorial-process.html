Editorial & testing

Our recorded experiments list the input, browser, test date, observed output, and limitations. Screenshots show actual tool runs. Data transformations are compared with fixed expected values; PDF downloads are reopened to check page count and dimensions. Automated browser and layout checks do not cover every device, document, or external service.

Published guides link to their fixtures and results so you can repeat the checks. Report a correction through the contact page with a small non-sensitive example. Automated checks and AI-assisted inspection do not establish a named human review; we do not invent individual reviewers or claim independent certification.
